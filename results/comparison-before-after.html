TLS 1.2 vs 1.3 Visualization Comparison

From Basic Bar Chart to Interactive Slope Chart

Before: Grouped Bar Chart Traditional

Standard grouped bar chart showing TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3 performance side-by-side. Requires visual comparison of bar heights to understand differences.

After: Slope Chart Improved

Interactive slope chart connecting TLS 1.2 to TLS 1.3 performance for each version. Slopes immediately show which protocol is faster and by how much.

Why the Slope Chart is Better

❌ Bar Chart Limitations

  • Requires mental comparison of bar heights
  • Difficult to see relationships between protocols
  • No indication of improvement vs regression
  • Less engaging visually
  • Doesn't leverage D3.js capabilities
  • Hard to identify patterns across versions

✅ Slope Chart Advantages

  • Immediate insight: Slope direction shows winner
  • Visual storytelling: Lines tell a clear story
  • Interactive: Hover for detailed information
  • More information: Shows % change explicitly
  • Pattern recognition: Easy to spot outliers
  • Professional: Modern, compelling design
