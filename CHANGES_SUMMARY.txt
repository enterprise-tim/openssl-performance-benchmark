TECHNICAL DETAILS
================================================================================

Y-Axis Scaling Algorithm:
  1. Find data range: min = -0.4%, max = +0.6%
  2. Get absolute maximum: 0.6%
  3. Add 25% padding: 0.6 × 1.25 = 0.75%
  4. Set symmetric domain: [-0.75%, +0.75%]

Result: Your 1% data range now fills ~80% of chart (not 5%)

Label Styling:
  - Font: 11px bold
  - Positive: #2f9e44 (dark green)
  - Negative: #c92a2a (dark red)
  - Precision: 1 decimal place
  - Format: "+0.6%", "-0.4%", etc.
